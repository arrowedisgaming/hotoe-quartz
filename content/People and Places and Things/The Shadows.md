# The Shadows

- Shadow-things that attacked the party in the sleeping-car corridor around midnight, 4-5 January 1893
- Vaguely humanoid but mostly flat and two-dimensional, with a distended maw, ragged wings, and long misshapen limbs
- Each wears **the face of a young woman** the party did not recognise
- Related to whatever rose out of [[Maria-Pook]], but where that was solid and corporeal, these are shadow
- **Immune to gunfire** — Amelia's bullet passed straight through and shattered a window. Their bite drains life force (4 damage a hit, no visible wound) and sanity. Seeing one costs a point of SAN even on a success
- **Hurt by light and fire.** Georgie cranked a gas lamp to full and the flame burned part of one away. Light, not bullets, is the answer
- Move in numbers and approach unseen out of darkness. At least two attacked Amelia; more were seen entering a cabin as the session ended
- Unknown whether [[Menkaph]] summons them, or whether the muffled scream Georgie heard means something aboard is feeding them
