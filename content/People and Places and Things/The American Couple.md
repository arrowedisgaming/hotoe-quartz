# The American Couple

- A young American husband and wife travelling on the Orient Express under [[Menkaph]]'s wing
- Menkaph is posing as their **spiritual adviser**. Per [[Bentley Burnham]], she likely went to Paris to collect them before joining the train
- The husband **drinks heavily and is afraid of her**
- Menkaph has **some hold over the wife** that the party has not yet identified
- Not yet seen face to face by the party. Whether they are victims, accomplices, or bait is unknown
