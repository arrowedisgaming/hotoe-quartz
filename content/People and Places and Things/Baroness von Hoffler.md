# Baroness von Hoffler

- Austrian noblewoman with a large estate and library outside Vienna
- Collector of books and occult objects: votive statues, strange garments, staffs, cult paraphernalia
- Styles herself a fighter against dark powers. [[Julia-Smith]] met her at a private gathering of occult-minded people at the estate
- Has funded Smith's research before, work the university would not pay for. Reputation for generosity
- **Bankrolling the party's expedition to Constantinople.** Money is no object for anything aimed at suppressing the cults or [[Menkaph]]
- Has donated good-quality books to the British Museum and is respected for her knowledge. Personal character hard to read from the catalogue
- Means to meet the party in person
- A wealthy stranger funding a dangerous trip and collecting cult artefacts. Worth and Georgie are both watching her. Smith vouches for her
