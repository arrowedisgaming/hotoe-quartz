# Roderick Barrington

- Captain Roderick Barrington. Named in the connection notes on several character sheets, finally on stage as a travelling companion
- Comes along on the Orient Express as a reserve investigator (a pre-generated PC held in case someone goes mad or dies). Shares a sleeping compartment with Polat
- Elizabeth's earlier framing was that he was unlikely to fight for the party. **In the event, he did (Session 4):** woke during the shadow attack, came out with his **sabre**, and slashed at [[The Shadows]] (a clumsy stroke that missed the thing and nearly clipped Amelia, gouging the floor). He held off his **shotgun**, sensibly, with Amelia in the line of fire
- Armed with a **pistol, a shotgun, and a sabre** (DEX 50)
- Saw the shadows for what they were and became a believer, losing a point of sanity
- Known to Amelia from Afghanistan, with a hint of old romance. Known to Saroch and Worth from the Oriental Club
- Leans "help" so far, if a clumsy one. Loyalties still not fully tested
