# Maria Pook

- Student of [[Professor Demir]] in Constantinople. Young woman, perhaps in her twenties. Ink-stained fingers
- Volunteered to follow [[Menkaph]] from Constantinople to London when Menkaph came into possession of [[The Blood Red Veil]]
- Surveilled Menkaph's cult as they staked out **3 Blithering Lane, Rotherhithe** (the veil collector's house). Kept a journal hidden in her mattress at her flat in Lambeth (4A, top floor)
- Decided to steal the Blood Red Veil from Menkaph's lodgings while Menkaph was in Rotherhithe. [[Professor Demir]] warned her against it. She was confident no one saw her
- [[Mrs-Leeds]] brought her to 5 Durward Street under the opium cover story. The veil was placed on her face and left to grow into her skin
- [[Julia-Smith]] identified her by name after the veil fell away at the moment of death
- **Died at the stroke of midnight, January 1, 1893.** Reanimated as a corporeal dark thing that bit half of Dr. Hobbs's face off. Amelia destroyed it with two revolver shots
- Body is headless, skin in tatters, left in situ at 5 Durward Street with Mrs. Grim
- A photograph recovered from her flat shows her as she was: a beloved daughter. Her parents sent it in an envelope
- Demir has not been informed of her death
