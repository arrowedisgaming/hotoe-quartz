# Henri Peters

- Conductor of the Orient Express on the party's run. A slim Belgian man, well turned out
- Welcomed the party aboard at Chalon-sur-Marne and showed them to their cabins. Polite, correct, attentive
- Says to call on him if anything goes wrong. Likely the party's main point of contact for the train itself
- Arrived during the shadow attack asking what was happening; Worth told him "intruders" (Session 4). How the railway, and Henri, will treat a shattered window and a screaming gunshot in the night is an open matter
